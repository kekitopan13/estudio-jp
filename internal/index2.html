Japanese Study Tools

Full index of the mini apps in this repository.

Public menu

  1. Match Up Public

    Match related pairs, such as kanji and readings, in short rounds. Builds quick recognition and strengthens links between two pieces of information.

    Input: Two fields per line, separated by a tab or semicolon. Example: 強調;きょうちょう

  2. Revisión global Public

    Review a full vocabulary list and self-rate each word’s reading and meaning. Identify what you know well and what needs more study.

    Input: word;reading;meaning, one entry per line.

  3. Deep Recall Public

    Practice vocabulary in both directions with multiple-choice and writing questions. Review incorrect answers in follow-up rounds to reinforce reading, written form, and meaning.

    Input: word;reading;meaning, one entry per line.

  4. Word Forge Public

    Learn from meaning cues by recognizing readings, building words kana by kana, and writing them from memory. Especially useful for hiragana vocabulary.

    Input: reading;meaning, one entry per line.

  5. Word Check Public

    Check whether you can recall a word’s reading from its meaning. Words correct on the first try are set aside; missed words get more practice.

    Input: reading;meaning, one entry per line. Uses kana readings.

  6. Flashcard review Public

    Review pairs with flashcards: recall the hidden item, reveal it, then rate how easy or difficult it was. Supports spaced review.

    Input: Two fields per line, separated by a semicolon. Example: 猫;ねこ

  7. Falsi Quizlet V2 Public

    Practice with multiple choice and optional writing. Choose what each column represents, which direction to test, and whether to use two or three data fields.

    Input: Two or three fields per line, separated by semicolons; assign each column to A, B, or C.

  8. Kanji vocabulary learning v.2 Public

    Study kanji vocabulary through guided exposure, word building, reading, and meaning checks. Cumulative challenge rounds help reveal what needs review.

    Input: word [tab] furigana [tab] meaning, one entry per line.

  9. Kanjis Confusos 2 Public

    Identify the hidden kanji in a word using its reading and meaning as clues. Choose practice with multiple attempts or a one-try assessment.

    Input: kanji;group;word;reading;meaning, one entry per line.

  10. Kanji Builder Public

    Build each vocabulary word by selecting its kanji in order, using the reading and optional meaning as clues. Practice written word composition.

    Input: word;reading;meaning, one entry per line. The word must contain at least one kanji.

Internal index only

  1. Kanjis Confusos Internal

    Practice distinguishing easily confused kanji by matching each character to its concept within similarity groups. Optional challenge rounds reinforce the differences.

    Input: kanji;group;concept, one entry per line. The group number identifies kanji to compare.

  2. Kanji vocabulary learning Older version

    Earlier version of the progressive kanji-vocabulary study app, with exposure, word building, reading, and meaning exercises.

    Input: Three tab-separated fields per line: word [tab] furigana [tab] meaning.

  3. Word Forge v2 Older version

    Earlier version of Word Forge for recognizing readings, building words kana by kana, and writing vocabulary from memory.

    Input: reading;meaning, one entry per line.
