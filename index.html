Japanese study tools

🧱 Apps for Building Knowledge
🔗
Match Up Multipurpose

Match related words, readings, concepts, or facts through quick pairing rounds. Strengthen recognition and active recall using any two connected sets of information.

Input: Two fields per pair, separated by a tab or semicolon. Example: 強調;きょうちょう

Learn more

How many fields does it require? 2 fields per pair.

Main use: Match related items through quick pairing rounds to strengthen recognition and active recall.

Can it be used for other subjects or languages? Yes; any two related concepts, words, translations, or facts can be matched.
🌊
Deep Recall Multipurpose

Strengthen vocabulary recall through multiple-choice quizzes and written practice in both directions. Revisit mistakes to reinforce readings, written forms, and meanings you tend to forget.

Input: word;reading;meaning, one entry per line.

Learn more

How many fields does it require? 3 fields per entry: Word, Reading, and Meaning. At least 2 entries are required.

Main use: Strengthen vocabulary recall, practice kanji readings, identify forgotten meanings, and reinforce weak words through multiple-choice and written recall.

Can it be used for other subjects or languages? Yes, for vocabulary in other languages, provided each entry has a written form, pronunciation, and meaning.
🔭
Word Forge Only for Japanese study

Learn Japanese vocabulary step by step by recognizing readings, assembling words kana by kana, and writing them from memory. Ideal for practicing hiragana and strengthening recall.

Input: reading;meaning, one entry per line.

Learn more

How many fields does it require? 2 fields per entry: reading and meaning.

Main use: Learn vocabulary through exposure, recognition, kana-by-kana construction, and writing from memory.

Can it be used for other subjects or languages? No; its mora-splitting and kana-construction exercises are designed specifically for Japanese.
✨
Falsi Quizlet V2 Multipurpose

Customize vocabulary practice with multiple-choice quizzes and written recall. Choose two or three fields, define what each represents, and select the direction of practice.

Input: Two or three fields per entry, separated by semicolons; assign each column to A, B, or C.

Learn more

How many fields does it require? 2 or 3 fields per entry, depending on the selected mode.

Main use: Practice configurable multiple-choice and written recall, choosing field roles and exercise direction.

Can it be used for other subjects or languages? Yes; it supports general paired or three-part information, though some options suit vocabulary best.
🌱
Kanji Vocabulary Learning v.2 Only for Japanese study

Learn Japanese vocabulary through guided kanji exposure, word construction, reading, and meaning checks. Cumulative challenges reinforce learned words and help identify what needs more practice.

Input: word [tab] furigana [tab] meaning, one entry per line.

Learn more

How many fields does it require? 3 fields per entry: word, furigana, and meaning.

Main use: Learn Japanese vocabulary through guided exposure, word construction, reading, meaning checks, and cumulative review.

Can it be used for other subjects or languages? No; its kanji construction and furigana-based exercises are Japanese-specific.
🔨
Kanji Builder Only for Japanese study

Practice reconstructing Japanese words by assembling their kanji in the correct order. Use readings and meanings as clues, then review mistakes and track your progress.

Input: word;reading;meaning, one entry per line. The word must contain at least one kanji.

Learn more

How many fields does it require? 3 fields per entry: word, reading, and meaning. The word must contain kanji.

Main use: Practice reconstructing kanji-containing words in order, using readings and meanings as clues.

Can it be used for other subjects or languages? No; it requires kanji-containing words and is designed specifically for Japanese.
🔎 Apps for Evaluating Knowledge
👾
Revisión global Multipurpose

Assess your knowledge across an entire vocabulary list by rating your recall of readings and meanings. Identify strengths, knowledge gaps, and words that need further review.

Input: word;reading;meaning, one entry per line.

Learn more

How many fields does it require? 3 fields per entry: word, reading, and meaning.

Main use: Self-assess vocabulary readings and meanings across a list to identify strengths and review gaps.

Can it be used for other subjects or languages? Yes; it can suit other languages as long as you have 3 pieces of information to input.
☑️
Word Check Only for Japanese study

Test whether you can recall Japanese readings from their meanings. Combine written answers and kana assembly, with targeted follow-up practice for words you struggle to remember.

Input: reading;meaning, one entry per line. Uses kana readings.

Learn more

How many fields does it require? 2 fields per entry: reading and meaning.

Main use: Recall kana readings from meanings, with writing, kana assembly, and targeted follow-up for missed words.

Can it be used for other subjects or languages? No; kana and mora-based exercises are built specifically for Japanese.
📝
Kanji Reading Practice Only for Japanese study

Evaluate whether you can recall and write a kanji word’s full reading in kana. Optionally build the reading mora by mora before the written assessment.

Input: word;reading;meaning, one entry per line. The word must contain at least one kanji.

Learn more

How many fields does it require? 3 fields per entry: word, reading, and meaning. The word must contain at least one kanji.

Main use: Evaluate recall of kanji vocabulary readings by asking you to write each complete reading in kana. An optional mora-by-mora construction round can precede the written evaluation.

Can it be used for other subjects or languages? No; its kanji prompts, kana readings, and mora-based practice are designed specifically for Japanese.
📇
Flashcard Review Multipurpose

Review vocabulary with simple flashcards: recall the hidden answer, reveal it, and rate how difficult it was to remember. Use your ratings to guide spaced review.

Input: Two fields per entry, separated by a semicolon. Example: 猫;ねこ

Learn more

How many fields does it require? 2 fields per entry.

Main use: Review vocabulary with flashcards, reveal answers, rate difficulty, and use ratings for spaced review.

Can it be used for other subjects or languages? Yes; it can review any two-sided facts, although its labels are Japanese-vocabulary-oriented.
😵‍💫
Kanjis Confusos 2 Only for Japanese study

Practice distinguishing easily confused kanji using grouped alternatives, example words, readings, and meanings. Switch between unlimited practice and one-attempt assessments to test your recognition.

Input: kanji;group;word;reading;meaning, one entry per line.

Learn more

How many fields does it require? 5 fields per entry: kanji, group, word, reading, and meaning.

Main use: Distinguish easily confused kanji using grouped alternatives, example words, readings, and meanings.

Can it be used for other subjects or languages? No; its kanji-discrimination exercises and required kanji field are Japanese-specific.
