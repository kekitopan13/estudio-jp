学習メニュー

Japanese study tools

🧱 Apps for Building Knowledge
🔗
Match Up Build associations through quick matching rounds. Connect related vocabulary, kanji, readings, or concepts and strengthen recognition through active recall.
Input: Two related fields per line, separated by a tab or semicolon. Example: 強調;きょうちょう
🌊
Deep Recall Build stronger vocabulary recall with multiple-choice and writing in both directions. Follow-up rounds revisit mistakes to reinforce readings, written forms, and meanings.
Input: word;reading;meaning, one entry per line.
🔭
Word Forge Build vocabulary step by step: recognize readings, assemble words kana by kana, then write them from memory. Especially useful for hiragana-focused study.
Input: reading;meaning, one entry per line.
✨
Falsi Quizlet V2 Build vocabulary recall with configurable multiple-choice and writing practice. Choose what each column represents, which direction to practice, and whether to use two or three fields.
Input: Two or three fields per line, separated by semicolons; assign each column to A, B, or C.
🌱
Kanji Vocabulary Learning v.2 Build kanji vocabulary through guided exposure, word construction, reading, and meaning checks. Cumulative challenge rounds reinforce words and reveal what needs review.
Input: word [tab] furigana [tab] meaning, one entry per line.
🔨
Kanji Builder Build written vocabulary by assembling each word’s kanji in order, using its reading and optional meaning as clues.
Input: word;reading;meaning, one entry per line. The word must contain at least one kanji.
🔎 Apps for Evaluating Knowledge
👾
Revisión global Evaluate a full vocabulary list by self-rating each word’s reading and meaning. Use the results to identify strengths and gaps for future study.
Input: word;reading;meaning, one entry per line.
☑️
Word Check Assess whether you can recall a reading from its meaning. First-try correct items are set aside; missed words receive targeted follow-up practice.
Input: reading;meaning, one entry per line. Uses kana readings.
📇
Flashcard Review Evaluate recall with flashcards: retrieve the hidden item, reveal it, then rate difficulty. Your ratings guide spaced review.
Input: Two fields per line, separated by a semicolon. Example: 猫;ねこ
😵‍💫
Kanjis Confusos 2 Evaluate whether you can distinguish a target kanji from similar alternatives, using its word, reading, and meaning as clues. Includes practice and one-try assessment modes.
Input: kanji;group;word;reading;meaning, one entry per line.
