Ejercicios de japonés

✨ Ejercicios en uso
🔗
Match Up Match related pairs, such as kanji and readings, in short rounds. Builds quick recognition and strengthens links between two pieces of information.
Input: Two fields per line, separated by a tab or semicolon. Example: 強調;きょうちょう
👾
Revisión global Review a full vocabulary list and self-rate each word’s reading and meaning. Identify what you know well and what needs more study.
Input: word;reading;meaning, one entry per line.
🌊
Deep Recall Practice vocabulary in both directions with multiple-choice and writing questions. Review incorrect answers in follow-up rounds to reinforce reading, written form, and meaning.
Input: word;reading;meaning, one entry per line.
🔭
Word Forge Learn from meaning cues by recognizing readings, building words kana by kana, and writing them from memory. Especially useful for hiragana vocabulary.
Input: reading;meaning, one entry per line.
☑️
Word Check Check whether you can recall a word’s reading from its meaning. Words correct on the first try are set aside; missed words get more practice.
Input: reading;meaning, one entry per line. Uses kana readings.
📇
Flashcard review Review pairs with flashcards: recall the hidden item, reveal it, then rate how easy or difficult it was. Supports spaced review.
Input: Two fields per line, separated by a semicolon. Example: 猫;ねこ
✨
Falsi Quizlet V2 Practice with multiple choice and optional writing. Choose what each column represents, which direction to test, and whether to use two or three data fields.
Input: Two or three fields per line, separated by semicolons; assign each column to A, B, or C.
🌱
Kanji vocabulary learning v.2. Study kanji vocabulary through guided exposure, word building, reading, and meaning checks. Cumulative challenge rounds help reveal what needs review.
Input: word [tab] furigana [tab] meaning, one entry per line.
🌀
Kanjis Confusos v.2 Identify the hidden kanji in a word using its reading and meaning as clues. Choose practice with multiple attempts or a one-try assessment.
Input: kanji;group;word;reading;meaning, one entry per line.
🔨
Kanji builder Build each vocabulary word by selecting its kanji in order, using the reading and optional meaning as clues. Practice written word composition.
Input: word;reading;meaning, one entry per line. The word must contain at least one kanji.
